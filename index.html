What We Do

From market assessment to qualified commercial opportunity.

Six practical services that turn U.S. market-entry intent into real activity — qualified prospects, real meetings, and progressing opportunities.

01 — Assessment

U.S. Market Entry & Opportunity Assessment

We help companies understand where their products and services fit within the U.S. market — identifying priority industries, customer segments, potential partners, market opportunities, and practical routes to market.

02 — Identification

Prospect Identification & Qualification

We research potential customers, partners, and other relevant organizations, identifying the decision-makers most relevant to each opportunity — prioritizing prospects on real fit, not contact volume.

03 — Outreach

Outreach & Lead Generation

We conduct targeted outreach to prospective customers and partners through appropriate business channels and industry networks — creating meaningful conversations with organizations that have a real need for your solution.

04 — Meetings

Business Meetings & Introductions

We help arrange and facilitate discussions between our clients and qualified U.S. prospects — supporting meeting preparation, introductions, and follow-up so each discussion has a clear commercial next step.

05 — Development

Partnership & Opportunity Development

Where there is strong fit, we help develop the opportunity further — including customer discovery, demonstrations, pilot discussions, proposals, local partnerships, and commercial negotiations.

06 — Reporting

Pipeline Management & Reporting

We maintain visibility over prospects, meetings, opportunities, and next actions. Clients receive regular updates on market feedback, business-development activity, and opportunities progressing through the pipeline.

How We Work

From market entry to commercial opportunity.

A six-stage engagement model designed to bring discipline and visibility to U.S. business development — so you always know what is happening, what is next, and what is moving.

  1. Understand

    We learn the client's solution, target customers, and U.S. objectives.

  2. Identify

    We research priority sectors, companies, decision-makers, and potential partners.

  3. Engage

    We conduct targeted outreach and qualify market interest.

  4. Connect

    We arrange discussions with relevant prospects and stakeholders.

  5. Develop

    We help progress qualified opportunities toward pilots, proposals, partnerships, or commercial discussions.

  6. Report

    We provide clear visibility of activity, market feedback, pipeline progress, and recommended next actions.
